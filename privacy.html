Privacy Policy

Last updated: 13 October 2026

Overview

MRCS Genius ("the App") is a mobile application designed to help surgical trainees prepare for the MRCS Part A examination. Your privacy is important to us. This policy explains what data we collect, how we use it, and your rights.

Data We Collect

The App stores all quiz progress, bookmarks, settings and statistics locally on your device only. We do not operate a backend server and do not collect, transmit, or store any personal data on remote servers.

  • No account registration required. The App does not ask for your name, email address, or any other personal identifier.
  • No analytics or tracking. We do not use analytics SDKs, advertising identifiers, or tracking cookies.
  • Purchase data. If you subscribe to MRCS Genius Premium, the transaction is processed entirely by Apple (via the App Store) or Google (via Google Play). We do not receive or store your payment details. RevenueCat, our subscription management partner, may receive a pseudonymous app user ID and purchase receipt to validate your entitlement; RevenueCat's privacy policy is available at revenuecat.com/privacy.

Data Stored on Your Device

The following data is stored locally using on-device storage (AsyncStorage) and never leaves your device:

  • Quiz scores and history
  • Bookmarked questions
  • Topic progress and statistics
  • App preferences (theme, settings)

Uninstalling the App permanently deletes all locally stored data.

Third-Party Services

  • RevenueCat — subscription management (pseudonymous ID and purchase receipt only).
  • Apple App Store / Google Play — in-app purchase processing.

We do not share any data with advertisers, data brokers, or any other third parties.

Children's Privacy

The App is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

Your Rights

Because all data is stored locally on your device, you have full control over it at all times. You can delete all App data by uninstalling the App or clearing its storage in your device settings.

Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Any changes will be reflected in the "Last updated" date above.

Contact

If you have questions about this Privacy Policy, please contact us at the email address provided on the App Store listing.
