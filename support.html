About the App

MRCS Genius is a comprehensive question bank app for MRCS Part A exam preparation, covering all 11 topics across Paper 1 (Applied Basic Sciences) and Paper 2 (Principles of Surgery in General). The app includes nearly 9,000 MCQs with detailed explanations, timed practice, mock exams, score tracking, and bookmarks.

Frequently Asked Questions

How do I restore my Premium subscription on a new device?

Go to Settings → Restore Purchases. Your subscription is tied to your Apple ID / Google account and can be restored on any device signed into the same account.

Will I lose my progress if I delete the app?

Yes. All progress, bookmarks, and quiz history are stored locally on your device. Uninstalling the app will permanently delete this data.

How do I cancel my subscription?

Subscriptions are managed through your Apple ID or Google Play account. On iOS: Settings → Apple ID → Subscriptions. On Android: Play Store → Subscriptions.

The app is not showing all questions.

Free users have access to 25 questions in the Anatomy topic. Upgrade to Premium to unlock all 8,974 questions across all 11 topics.
